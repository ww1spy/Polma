# Out-of-sample portfolio test: $500, May 1–31, v9 rules

Owner-requested. Full engine semantics: hourly entry signals, real
ask fills, fees, 2.5% sizing, exposure/position caps, daily-loss and
15% drawdown halts, tp@0.98, ask-side disaster stop. The v9 include-list
was selected on Jun 10–Jul 8 data, so May is genuinely out-of-sample.

## Result: $500 → $433.24 (−13.4%). Drawdown halt fired in week 3.

- 189 trades, 91% win rate — and still down 13.4%: losses cluster.
- Weekly equity: $505.72 → $516.07 → $433.22 (halt) → flat.
- Family P&L: RT **+$8.64**, TRUMPSAY +$0.42, MLB **−$30.92**, WTI **−$44.91**.
- Sensitivity without WTI: −1.1%. Promotion scenario (5%/25% halt): −0.9%.

## Verdicts
- **WTI: REJECTED.** The "mechanism unclear — provisional" flag was
  correct. Its Jun–Jul +1.9% was regime luck.
- **MLB: DEMOTED.** +2.2% in-sample, −$31 OOS. The favorite-longshot
  mechanism is real in the literature but not reliably harvestable here
  at these prices net of fees.
- **RT and TRUMPSAY: SURVIVE** — positive in both independent periods,
  strongest mechanisms. WT20 kept provisionally (thin May sample).
- **Honest bottom line: the live strategy's demonstrated OOS edge is
  ≈ zero net of fees outside RT/TRUMPSAY.** Live trades only what
  survived both periods (v10); demoted families keep being measured in
  the aggr paper book at zero real-money cost.

## Methodology notes
- Portfolio-level simulation exposes what per-trade tables hide: loss
  clustering (the week-3 crash), halt interactions, capital contention.
- A 91%-win, negative-ROI month is exactly the failure mode M2 predicts:
  favorites' rare losses arrive correlated (same regime), not i.i.d.
