# Sizing Monte Carlo — what does widening risk tolerance buy? (2026-07-09)

Owner asked whether a wider risk tolerance, played smartly, likely nets
positive. Method: day-block bootstrap over the live include-list's 60-day
per-trade returns (407 trades / 48 active days, mean +2.45%/trade, fees in;
favorite band on RT/TRUMPSAY/WT20 + RT last-mile), 30-day paths, 4,000
paths/config, sequential compounding at fraction f, drawdown-halt modeled
(a halted path stops trading). Script: session scratchpad `sizing_mc.py`.

## Results — 30-day horizon, halt at 15% drawdown

| f/trade | median | 5th pct | 95th pct | P(halt) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.5% (current) | +16.6% | +7.6% | +26.2% | 0.0% |
| 5% | +35.5% | +14.9% | +60.2% | 0.2% |
| 10% | +81.1% | −4.7% | +153.3% | **23.4%** |
| 15% | +17.7% | −12.8% | +216.8% | **93.8%** |
| 25% | +11.6% | −18.8% | +269.3% | **99.2%** |

With the halt loosened to 25%: f=10% → median +83%, P(halt) 0.5%;
f=15% → median +141% but P(halt) 12.6% and 5th pct ≈ 0; f=25% → 93% halt.

**Shape of the answer:** growth rises with f until halt probability takes
over, then collapses — at 15%+ per trade most paths freeze in a halt long
before the big wins arrive. The halt-adjusted optimum sits near 5% today
and near 10% only if the halt is loosened to 25%. 25% per trade is wealth
destruction at any halt setting.

## RT last-mile tail sensitivity (f=10%, halt 25%)

119/119 wins bounds the true loss rate only to <2.5% (rule of three, 95%).
Injecting synthetic full losses: tail 0% → median +82%; 1% → +69%;
2.5% → +50% with 5th pct −17.6%. The edge survives its own uncertainty
band, but the downside tail widens fast — reason to keep last-mile at
standard sizing, not to over-size the "sure thing."

## Why the medians overstate live reality (read before acting)

1. **In-sample edge:** the +2.45%/trade mean comes from the same 60-day
   window that selected these families (the MLB/WTI lesson). Forward edge
   is likely lower; the RELATIVE ordering across f is the robust takeaway,
   not the absolute medians.
2. **Trade flow:** the backtest finds ~8.5 entries/active day; the live
   book at hourly cadence with cooldowns and the correlation guard sees a
   fraction of that. Scale the absolute numbers down accordingly.
3. Concurrency ignored: at f≥10% the 60% exposure cap and 8-position cap
   would throttle flow before the MC's assumptions do.
4. Day-blocks preserve within-day loss clustering but not multi-day bad
   regimes (WNBA-style −8% half-periods).

## Policy conclusion (owner decision required)

- Raising live sizing 2.5% → **5%** (the hard cap in risk_limits.yaml
  already permits 5%) roughly doubles expected growth with P(halt) ≈ 0.
  This grants the existing 50-trade promotion gate's target early, on
  backtest+MC evidence instead of live evidence.
- Going past 5% is only defensible with the halt loosened to ~25%, and
  should wait for live confirmation (e.g. 25 settled live trades, ≤2
  losses) — the same one-period-evidence trap has burned us twice.
- Fraction-raising is the weaker lever anyway: more capital or more
  validated families raise dollar P&L linearly without touching risk of
  ruin. At 2.5% on $103 the MC median is ≈ +$17/month; the identical risk
  profile on a $500 bankroll is ≈ +$80/month.
