## Policy (rev. 2026-09-28)
- PROMOTE-CANDIDATE needs n ≥ 100, both halves > 0, and the Bonferroni-corrected lower bound > 0. Promotion still needs a human.
- LIVE DEMOTE (ROI < 0 or both halves < 0) is conservative and may be applied by the weekly session. UNPROVEN is informational.
- Maker rows are research: live execution is taker until a maker paper book confirms fills at live fidelity.
- LB is also capped by the ROI after one more full-stake loss (tail guard: a family with no losses in-sample has a meaningless sample SE).
- Fast-family DEMOTE (90% upper bound < 0) / REVIEW flags apply to the eth15 PAPER book; the control row is informational.
- This script never edits rules itself.
