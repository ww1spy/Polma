- **Date closed:** 2026-10-11

- **Strategy:** favorite_discount (rules v14)

- **Context at exit:** bid 0.13 / ask 0.15 vs entry 0.950

## What the rules saw at entry
An in-play favorite at 0.95 in a World Championship of Legends match, which is a
retired-players exhibition T20. The position was standard sized at about 2% of equity.

## Why it lost
The match swung within the hour (0.95 → bid 0.13), and the stop fired near the bottom.
Ex-pro exhibition cricket has much higher variance than its price implied. A late
collapse in T20 can reverse a 95% position within a few overs. The thesis was wrong
because 0.95 overstated how settled the match was. Sizing worked as intended: the
loss was capped at about $9.

## Rule change proposed
None yet; one loss is not a sample. Watch the `CRICWCL` / legends-exhibition
cricket results on Polymarket. If the next revalidation shows a negative
lower bound on this family, propose excluding it. rules/*.yaml is not edited here.
