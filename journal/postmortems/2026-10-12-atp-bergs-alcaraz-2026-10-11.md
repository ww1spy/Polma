## Why it lost
An upset. A 0.93 favorite should lose about 7% of the time, and this was one of
those times. The stop-loss never fired: the match started and finished between
hourly cycles, so no mark below the stop was ever seen, and the position went
straight to settlement at 0. That is the known gap with an hourly cycle on
in-play tennis: the stop cannot protect a match that resolves in under an hour.

32 wins and 7 losses since 9/28. The loss count is still small, but the 60-day
tennis backtest offered earlier would show whether the edge covers losses
