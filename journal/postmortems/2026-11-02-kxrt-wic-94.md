## What the rules saw at entry
Limit-order (maker) book. Resting bid placed 2026-11-01 23:21 at 0.89 (book 0.88/0.90),
filled 11/11 at 00:21 on 11/02, ~15h before the 2026-11-02 15:00 close. Same film, same book:
two days earlier it held "Above 92" **Yes** (filled 0.89, took profit +$0.97 at 0.98 on 11/01) —
i.e. the market then priced a score above 92 as very likely, and this bet was that the score
would *not* clear 94, a 2-point band above a level the market already expected to be cleared.

## Why it lost
Thesis wrong / repricing: within ~5h of the fill the No collapsed to bid 0.22 / ask 0.33
(the market moved to expecting a score above 94, presumably on new reviews). The stop fired at
the 05:20 cycle and sold at the bid 0.22 — the ask-based trigger is designed to avoid phantom
stops, but the actual fill still crosses a wide (0.11) spread on exit.

Rotten Tomatoes bands near the current score are adjacent-strike bets on a number that moves
as reviews arrive; a fill at 0.89 one strike above a level the book had just been paid on is a
correlated, thin-margin position.

Limit-order book KXRT record to date: 26W +$13.29 / 1L -$7.51. Whole limit-order book:
74W +$40.96 / 6L -$47.03, net -$6.07.

## Rule change proposed
Not applied (owner decision):
1. For KXRT, skip strikes within ~3 points of a strike the book has held/settled in the last
   few days (adjacent-band guard), or require a larger distance between strike and the
   implied current score.
2. Otherwise acceptable variance for the family — one loss in 27 — but note the payoff shape:
   one stop costs ~6 average wins.
